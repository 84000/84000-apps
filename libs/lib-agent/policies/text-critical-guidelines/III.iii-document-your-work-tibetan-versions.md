## iii. Document your work

Once again, when working in this way, it is important to clearly and succinctly describe one’s methodology in the introduction, record in notes the variant readings and one’s preferred reading, and explain why this selection was made in each individual case. One’s rationale should be generally consistent with the explanation in the introduction and applied consistently throughout the translation. In cases when a different rationale is applied, an explanation as to why should be provided in an attached note.
