# The analytics pass

Beyond the structural and catalog record, assemble the following for the
translator's use.

Each item must be **sourced** — from the retrieved text itself, the catalog, the
84000 Studio glossary and bibliography tools, or the translator's own input.
Never from unverified memory. Where an item cannot be sourced, record it as an
open question rather than as a finding.

## Genre

Sūtra, tantra, dhāraṇī, śāstra, vinaya text, and so on, as indicated by the
catalog and by the text's own self-description — its title or its colophon.

## Register

An initial read of whether the text is pitched at a general or listener audience
or uses dense technical and philosophical language, per
`translator-guidelines/I.E-target-readership.md`.

State this as a **first impression for the translator to confirm**, not a
settled judgment.

## Precedent translations

Any existing 84000 published translation of this Toh number, and any other
English or other-language translations the translator has flagged or that are
found via the 84000 Studio search tools.

Record title, translator, and year. **Do not assert a translation exists without
a locatable citation.** If none is found, "none located" is the finding — record
it explicitly rather than leaving the section empty.

## Terminological field

The technical or domain vocabulary the text appears to draw on — Abhidharma
categories, tantric sādhana terminology, Vinaya procedural vocabulary — to flag
early which glossary sections and reference works will matter most at Stage 1.

## Parallel witnesses

Which parallel versions are known to exist for this work: other Kangyur/Tengyur
recensions, Sanskrit witnesses, Chinese canonical parallels (with Taishō number
where known), Dunhuang manuscripts, or Pedurma-documented variants.

Record only what is attested in the catalog, in the source tools, or in
translator input. Mark anything uncertain as a question rather than a fact.

This item records that witnesses **exist**. It is not itself a comparison of
them — that is `collation.md`, and it happens only when the translator has asked
for it.
